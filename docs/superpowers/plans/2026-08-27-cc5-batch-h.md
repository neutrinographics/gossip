## Design decisions (pre-filled; binding unless Joel vetoes)

- **DH1 — OBS-8 dispositioned, not executed.** `ChannelAggregate` stays exported: repository implementers must name it (OpenDoorApp's `HiveChannelRepository` does — findById/save/reconstitute). Close OBS-8 in the addendum with that rationale plus one doc line on the aggregate stating its SPI role and that mutation outside `ChannelService` bypasses event emission.
- **DH2 — `BufferOverflowOccurred` deleted, `StreamCompacted` wired.** Both are never-emitted events; the difference is that compaction genuinely happens (an observable lifecycle apps want — the chat example already has a logging case waiting for it), while no buffer-overflow semantics exist anywhere to emit. Delete the phantom, wire the real one from the compaction path with the real `CompactionResult`.
- **DH3 — `entriesForAuthorAfter` deleted from the `EntryRepository` interface** (production-dead in gossip; OpenDoorApp's Hive implementation carries a dead override that breaks loudly at ref-bump — CHANGELOG-recorded, consistent with the accepted migration).
- **DH4 — `FailureDetector.timePort` and `.rttTracker`:** whichever of the two has test/harness readers becomes `@visibleForTesting` (the E6 convention); one with zero readers anywhere goes private. Grep decides; report states which.
- **DH5 — MIN-8 both halves fixed:** the constructor defensively copies the caller's map AND normalizes explicit zeros out (so `VersionVector({a: 0})` equals `VersionVector.empty`, matching the read semantics where a missing author reads 0). Equality is a value-object contract; two representations of the same value must not compare unequal.
- **DH6 — MIN-13:** `InMemoryTimePort.schedulePeriodic` honors its interval under `advance()` (fires once per elapsed interval, not once per advance call). Existing tests that relied on the divergence are findings to fix at the call site, not reasons to keep the lie.
